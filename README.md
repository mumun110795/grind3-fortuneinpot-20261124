## About this entry

The fortune is told to one particular plant on one particular windowsill, in the calm second-person voice of a fortune cookie: a future stated as fact, not advice. Its whole horizon is a plant's real one — the watering can that returns on Sunday, the slow turn toward the light as spring comes, and the cat who shares the sill. The small joke is the last clause: the best luck a plant can be promised is not riches, but the cat finally napping somewhere else. No doom, no withering — just a bigger, sunnier season ahead.
